For a top, twice the flat chest width gives its unstretched chest circumference. The skirt and trouser waist and hip values are full circumferences. Each trouser thigh and cuff value is the width of one leg laid flat. The Mermaid Long Skirt chart also contains a separate wearer-waist range. The simulation uses the garment's waist column.

Top lengths follow the fabric along the center back to the hem. On both tees the sleeve measurement includes half the shoulder span. Trouser inseams follow each inside leg from crotch to cuff. The three US trouser charts omit rise, so the patterns estimate it separately for the ankle pants, high-rise wide trousers and joggers.

The historical product photographs guide the width and depth of the ribbed tank’s scoop, its curved hem, the tee cuffs and the leg outlines. On-model photographs of the corresponding AIRism oversized tee and cotton Smart Ankle Pants styles give additional references for shoulder drop, sleeve reach and ankle break. Published US chart values above supply the dimensions for each historical style. Finished dimensions already include the design's ease. For example, the women's medium ribbed tank measures 31 inches around the chest and the medium seamless tank measures 37 inches. Necklines, fabric behavior, and other details outside these chart measurements are modeled approximations. A simulation cannot establish what size will fit a particular person.
